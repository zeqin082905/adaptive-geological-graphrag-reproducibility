# Data availability and access restrictions

## Public material

This repository contains source code, configuration parameters, evaluation utilities,
aggregate results, data schemas, and a fictional demonstration corpus. The fictional
corpus supports functional verification of the processing and retrieval pipeline.

## Restricted material

The main study used geological survey and exploration reports held by third-party or
institutional archives. The authors are not authorized to redistribute those documents,
derived full-text passages, document-level indexes, embeddings, or the expert-validated
question-answer set. These materials are therefore not present in this repository or its
Git history.

The restriction prevents public reproduction of the exact manuscript scores, but does not
prevent inspection and functional execution of the implemented method. Access to the
restricted material is governed by the relevant data-owning institutions and cannot be
granted unilaterally by the repository maintainers.

## Suggested manuscript statement

> The source code, experiment configurations, evaluation scripts, aggregate results, and a
> synthetic demonstration dataset are available in the accompanying public repository.
> The geological survey reports and expert-validated question-answer dataset used in the
> main experiments cannot be publicly redistributed because they are subject to
> third-party ownership, archive-use agreements, and institutional access restrictions.
> The synthetic dataset reproduces the input schema and supports functional execution of
> the proposed pipeline. Access to the restricted materials is governed by the relevant
> data-owning institutions and cannot be granted unilaterally by the authors.
